# How to Safely Merge Bank Statements into One PDF Privately

When applying for a mortgage, personal loan, apartment lease, or international visa, lenders and immigration authorities almost always demand **3 to 6 consecutive months of bank statements submitted as a single consolidated PDF file**.

However, bank statements contain sensitive private information: your home address, account numbers, salary deposits, transaction records, and tax IDs. Uploading these documents to random third-party converters is a major privacy and security risk.

Here is how you can merge your financial statements into a single, clean PDF file **100% locally on your own computer or phone**.

---

## Why You Should Never Upload Bank Statements to Unknown Cloud Services

Most free online PDF websites upload your files directly to their remote web servers. Once on their server, your documents may be:
- Cached or logged in temporary server folders.
- Subject to data breaches or unauthorized access.
- Scraped for training data or commercial analytics.

Under financial data protection regulations (such as GDPR), sensitive financial documents should only be handled with end-to-end security.

**KeepPDF solves this problem entirely**: Our merging engine operates using client-side WebAssembly directly inside your browser. Your statements **never touch our servers or leave your device**.

### Step 1: Download Your Statements in Chronological Order
Log in to your online banking portal and download each month's statement as an individual PDF (e.g., `January_2026.pdf`, `February_2026.pdf`, `March_2026.pdf`).
