# How to Compress PDF to 200KB or 100KB for Visa and Job Portals

When applying for a visa, submitting university entrance papers, or applying for jobs through applicant tracking systems (ATS), you will frequently run into strict file size limits. Many government and corporate portals strictly reject any PDF larger than **200 KB** or **100 KB**.

Here is how you can easily compress any PDF document to meet these stringent limits without making your text fuzzy or unreadable.

---

## Why Do Government and Visa Portals Enforce a 200KB Limit?

Government portals—such as the US DS-160/CEAC consular system, UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI), Schengen visa appointment systems, and university admissions—process millions of uploads every week. 

To conserve server storage and ensure fast database queries, they limit uploads:
- **100 KB – 200 KB**: Supporting documents, passport photo scans, and birth certificates.
- **500 KB – 1 MB**: Multipage employment contracts, diplomas, and official statements.
- **2 MB – 5 MB**: Complete tax bundles and business registrations.

If your file is even 205 KB, the upload will fail with an error like *"File size exceeds the allowable limit"*.

## Key Tips to Avoid Blurry Text at Small File Sizes

1. **Keep Text Vectorized**: Whenever possible, generate PDFs directly from Microsoft Word or Google Docs using *"Save As PDF"* rather than scanning printed paper. Vector text stays infinitely sharp regardless of file compression.
2. **Crop Unnecessary White Margins**: If you scanned a document with a wide white border, cropping the excess canvas cuts file size by up to 20%.
3. **Use Black & White for Scans**: If color isn't strictly requested by the portal, grayscale scans compress down to less than half the size of full 24-bit color scans.
