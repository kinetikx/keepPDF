# How to Merge PDF Files Online — A Complete Guide

Merging PDF files is one of the most common document tasks, whether you're combining reports for work, assembling academic papers, or organizing personal documents. In this guide, we'll walk you through everything you need to know about merging PDFs online.

## Why Merge PDF Files?

There are countless situations where you need to combine multiple PDFs into a single document:

- **Business Reports**: Combine monthly department reports into one comprehensive file
- **Academic Papers**: Merge your research, bibliography, and appendices
- **Legal Documents**: Assemble contract pages, signatures, and exhibits
- **Personal Organization**: Combine receipts, tickets, or travel documents
